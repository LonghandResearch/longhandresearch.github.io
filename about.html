How the library is kept.

Longhand is a library of independent investment research: companies, the sectors they compete in and the economies around them.

How the reports are built

Each report sets out the thesis, the valuation and the main risks, and each is published here whole, to read at leisure or keep.

An initiation runs from the executive summary through the company profile, the industry backdrop, the financial history, forecasts and valuation, to the risks and the rating. The historical statements and the working behind the numbers sit in the appendices, so each figure can be traced back to the company’s filings.

Prices, targets and ratings are as they stood on the day a report was published, and they are not revised afterwards. A change of view is published as an update.

Rating key

Ratings follow the upside to the target price at publication.

BUY
Upside above 15%
HOLD
Between 10% downside and 15% upside
SELL
Downside of more than 10%

Report types

Initiation
The first full report on a company, with a rating and target.
Update
A change in view after results or news.
Sector
Companies in one industry, side by side.
Macro
