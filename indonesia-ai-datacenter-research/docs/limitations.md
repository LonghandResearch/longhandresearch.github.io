# Limits and required diligence

1. **No current national metered total.** Public evidence does not establish an exhaustive Indonesian data-center IT capacity census, energy consumption total or direct consumptive-water total at the research cutoff. Official MW headlines and industry metro IT MW have different scopes. The dated metro estimate and partial operator inventory remain separate. Actual national AI-specific use is unavailable.
2. **Partial project coverage and overlapping announcements.** Disclosed facility pages, campus ultimate capacities, construction phases and portfolios can overlap. Known overlaps/conflicts are retained and excluded as needed. Large cloud providers often disclose investments and region openings without MW. Phase timing does not prove commissioning; contradictory commissioning claims are retained for review.
3. **Nameplate versus load.** Utilization is assumed average electrical load factor. Commercial occupancy, leased MW, GPU utilization and installed/nameplate MW are different. A full-year annualized run rate overstates the commissioning year's annual energy when capacity starts during the year.
4. **PUE comparability.** Measured annual PUE, design PUE at full load, regional operator averages and old Indonesian facility disclosures are not interchangeable. New-build high PUE is not a maximum for legacy assets. Domestic designs do not by themselves prove actual annual performance.
5. **WUE boundary.** Some operator metrics measure withdrawals or unspecified water use; others consumption. Source boundaries remain labeled. Consumptive scenario WUE does not establish Indonesian actual usage. Zero cooling consumption excludes sanitary and indirect supply-chain water. Site-specific cooling, blowdown/reuse and wastewater return information is absent.
6. **Tariff contracts.** Published PLN active-energy tariffs exclude premiums, demand/minimum bills, time-of-use, taxes, reactive penalties, backup fuel and private contracts. A qualifying I-4 contract is an assumption, not a confirmed classification for all data centers. Reactive charges must not become active-energy rates.
7. **Water utility geography and billing.** PAM JAYA applies to Jakarta, not the country. Historical Batam schedules may differ from current contracts. Bekasi/Cikarang/Karawang estate water contracts and many utility tariffs remain undisclosed or unverified. Modeled consumptive m3 is priced as a scenario; billed withdrawal may be higher. Marginal tariffs exclude lower-band allocation/fixed charges.
8. **Climate coverage.** Verified station records are used only at their stated location/time. Not all target locations have accessible comparable monthly records. Missing wet bulb remains missing. No unsupported wet-bulb calculation or physical relationship linking climate/PUE/WUE is fitted.
9. **Local infrastructure headroom.** National annual TWh comparisons do not establish substation/transmission capacity, queue positions, redundant connection reliability, water permissions or dry-season yield. Batam/Bintan and Java-Bali grids are not fungible. Grid losses, reserve margin, outage probability, hourly renewable matching and on-site backup are outside the simple energy-equivalent generation calculation.
10. **Forecast basis.** Official/industry anchors are preserved with their geography, load basis and date. Analyst resource/capacity deviations and future national denominator growth are scenarios. None of the aggressive outlook parameters is represented as a committed forecast. The model does not assume all new capacity is AI.
11. **Source access and reproducibility.** Source URLs may change or reject automated requests. Curated numerical records, paraphrased evidence, document/table locators and hashes reproduce this version offline. Full live document archives are optional and not republished. Assertions not supported by exact evidence remain missing. Refreshes require analyst review; source scraping is not unattended fact approval.
12. **Excel verification.** Python/OpenXML-generated formula caches describe the initial values and Excel recalculation is requested on open. Independent calculation/preview checks are documented in `outputs/workbook_validation.json`. Native Microsoft Excel interaction is not claimed unless explicitly recorded there. After changing Excel assumptions, use automatic calculation or Calculate Now. Python charts and research summary refresh only by rerunning Python with the corresponding YAML changes; Excel edits do not write back to YAML.

These gaps constrain what the project can conclude. They do not authorize filling missing national totals, facility water use, tariffs, hardware counts or wet-bulb observations with invented values.
