IT kWh = IT MW × 1,000 × average load factor × 8,760 hours.
Facility kWh = IT kWh × PUE.
Water m³ = IT kWh × consumptive WUE ÷ 1,000.

Annual steady-state operation is assumed. A monthly value is the annual result divided by twelve. Zero cooling WUE excludes sanitary use and water consumed in electricity generation. The low PUE and zero WUE preset is an exploratory combination that requires an engineering design check.

01

Power has to reach the site.

Check connection capacity, transformer headroom and delivery timing. Annual national energy does not establish local grid availability.

02

Water needs a local balance.

Check dry-season allocation, permitted intake, discharge and reuse. A low water bill can still sit beside a difficult supply constraint.

03

Cooling choices have to work together.

Efficient server cooling does not by itself determine outdoor water use. PUE and WUE need to be tested against the actual heat-rejection design.
