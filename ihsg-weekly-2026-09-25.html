01 / EXECUTIVE THESIS

A rebound without follow-through

The IHSG fell 3.09% while IDX recorded Rp3.16tn of foreign net selling across all five sessions. Thursday alone contributed Rp1.49tn of that total. Wednesday’s 97.868-point rebound after BI held rates was followed by a 133.020-point decline through Friday. The price recovery lacked flow confirmation. [1] [3] [6]

The Fed’s 16 September hike framed the external rate backdrop; BI’s 23 September hold maintained its currency-stability stance. JISDOR rose 0.97% between the two Fridays, increasing the currency hurdle for an unhedged dollar investor. These observations align with risk reduction but cannot apportion the index decline causally among policy, FX and company news. [4] [5] [6]

LONGHAND RETURN LAB / JISDOR BASELINE AND SCENARIOS

The dollar investor’s extra loss

The BI reference rate moved from Rp17,745 to Rp17,917 per dollar between the two Fridays. With the IHSG price return, that implies an indicative −4.02% USD return. Move either control to test a different assumption. [4]

Indicative unhedged USD price return

RUSD = (1 + RIDR) / (1 + ΔUSD/IDR) − 1

Observed endpoints and conversion

For initial dollar wealth W and USD/IDR rates S₀ and S₁, final dollar wealth is W × S₀ × (1 + RIDR) ÷ S₁. Since S₁/S₀ = 1 + ΔUSD/IDR, dividing final wealth by W gives the formula above. Inputs are decimal returns inside the formula.

The five rows hold the observed IHSG return constant and vary the currency move; the highlighted row uses BI’s endpoints.

USD/IDR changeUSD returnInterpretation

JISDOR samples 09:00–15:00 WIB and is published at 15:15. Its timing is not synchronised with the equity closing auction. The −4.02% is therefore a reference-rate proxy, not an executable close-to-close USD return. It excludes dividends, fees, taxes, hedging and tracking costs. Other rows are scenarios. [4]

Foreign selling persisted across all five sessions

IDX reports Rp3,155.08bn of foreign net selling for 21–25 September. It was the third consecutive negative week, bringing the three-week sum to Rp9.46tn of net selling. Daily statistics are negative every day and sum exactly to the latest weekly figure. Thursday carried 47.3% of that week’s net outflow. These figures measure traded stock value by investor classification, not capital leaving Indonesia or a direct contribution to the IHSG. [1] [2] [3]

The weekly report separately records Rp53.66tn of total regular-market turnover and Rp6.23tn of negotiated-market turnover. Its foreign-net figure does not allocate investors’ purchases or sales by venue, so a regular-versus-negotiated foreign-flow split cannot be calculated from these files. [1]

03 / SECTOR ROTATION & THEMATIC ENGINE

Ten sectors declined for the week

IDX’s weekly sector indices show only consumer cyclicals finishing positive, by 0.02%. That gain is too small to establish meaningful leadership. Industrials fell 5.48%, infrastructure 4.10% and technology 3.96%. Materials and energy also finished lower, despite their midweek rebound. Price performance establishes where risk was borne; it does not identify foreign accumulation or “smart money.” [1]

Higher share volume alongside 21.32% lower traded value is consistent with a shift toward lower-priced shares, lower prices or both. The ratio does not reveal investor intent. For a durable rebound, watch whether turnover recovers while foreign selling eases and more sector indices participate.

04 / HIGH-CONVICTION STOCK THESIS

Three names drove a third of the index loss

IDX attributes 66.10 negative IHSG points to BYAN, BBRI and BMRI combined, against the index’s net 199.267-point fall. This is roughly one-third of the net decline before gains elsewhere offset losses. The following catalysts are conditions to test, not confirmed developments or target-price calls. [1]

BYAN

Coal exposure needs a fundamental check

BYAN fell 12.48% and subtracted 28.09 IHSG points, the largest negative contribution in the IDX table. A recovery case needs evidence from coal prices, sales volumes and cash generation. None of those issuer drivers is established by the supplied market statistics.

BBRI

Credit quality is the re-rating test

BBRI fell 4.83% and subtracted 23.33 points. The next operating test is whether funding costs and provisions leave earnings and return on equity resilient. The index attribution does not identify foreign selling in BBRI specifically.

BMRI

Watch margin and funding resilience

BMRI fell 4.23% and subtracted 14.68 points. A stronger thesis requires stable low-cost deposits, NIM and credit costs alongside a broader market recovery. Price weakness alone does not establish a valuation discount.

Price moves and IHSG-point contributions are IDX observations [1]. The operating checkpoints are analytical conditions. No current earnings result, valuation multiple, company-specific foreign flow or verified issuer catalyst is asserted.

Tactical stance

Defensive tilt; conditional, staged accumulation. Add risk only as price recovery gains sector participation and daily foreign selling eases. For unhedged dollar investors, test the FX hurdle against an executable rate before acting. A lower index level alone is insufficient evidence of value.
