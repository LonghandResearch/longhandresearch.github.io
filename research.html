      Source notebook

      Add original links and the passage or figures they support. “Checked” records your manual review; it does not fetch or verify a source.

        Working draft

        World produces planning text. Replace it with researched prose before submitting for review.

        Review and approval

        Only you change the editorial status. Approval needs a research draft, a checked source and a review note. Editing evidence or prose resets approval to Draft.

        Publication handoff

        Approve the research, prepare its final PDF, then use Library's Add report / Publish tools. Connect the resulting record here and reload projects & reports after publication.
