    Five roles and required deliverables
      Continue in World

      Source notebook

      Add original links and the passage or figures they support. “Checked” records your manual review; it does not fetch or verify a source.

        Reasoning record

        Explain what you believe, why, and what could change it. Keep observations and estimates distinct from interpretation. Save incomplete work whenever needed. Research principles

        Evidence for this thesis

        Link a source already in the notebook to the thesis. Describe the passage and its relevance, including evidence that qualifies or challenges the position.

          Working draft

          World produces planning text. Replace it with researched prose before submitting for review.

          This preserves your existing prose and adds the open reasoning record. It does not mark planning text as researched or grant approval.

          Review and approval

          Approval needs a researched draft, a checked source, a reasoning record with linked evidence, and your manual review. Editing evidence, prose or reasoning clears the current review. These checks record your judgment; the software does not verify the claims.

          Manual review checklist

          A manually supplied attribution, not an authenticated account.

          Publication handoff

          Approve the research, prepare its final PDF, then use Library's Add report / Publish tools. Connect the resulting record here and reload projects & reports after publication.
