   metal miners on Monday. The row now counts them as evidence for the 70% remark, with PTBA and ITMG
   against.

   its reason for the third-quarter portfolio deficit (less money into government bonds). Both are in now.

9. "Asia had its worst week since 2020" became "headed for", as both CNBC and CNBC Indonesia (citing Reuters)
