## 8. Method for the argument

The explanations table follows the analysis of competing hypotheses in Heuer, *Psychology of
Intelligence Analysis*, chapter 8: list every plausible explanation, test each against the evidence
that would rule it out, and keep the one with the least evidence against it. Two explanations
(foreign return, global rally) fail on evidence. The surviving one (issuer and state buying) is
labelled with its weakness: no amounts. The paragraphs follow the claim, reasons, evidence and
acknowledgement pattern of Booth and colleagues, *The Craft of Research*, and the scenario check
follows Galef's *Scout Mindset*: last week's planning case is reported as wrong on timing, in the
judgment section, not buried.
