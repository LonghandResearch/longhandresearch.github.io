## 2. Extraction and reconciliation

```
python -I scripts/extract-ihsg-week.py "<folder>" weekly/market-2026-10-23.json --prior-close 5185.105 --render <scratch folder>
```

The script records file hashes and every daily and weekly figure the edition
needs, then fails loudly unless each check passes: first Previous equals
5,185.105, Previous chains day to day, the YTD foreign net chains, the daily
RG, TN and NG tables sum to weekly page 4, the weekly close matches, and buy
less sell matches the printed weekly net. It was tested on 24 October against
the audited 12 to 16 October files (23 of 23 checks, every shared field
identical to `weekly/market-2026-10-16.json`) and the 5 to 9 October files
(23 of 23, closes and nets equal to the published edition).

Breadth is read by eye from the rendered weekly page 4 and Friday daily page 5,
by colour against the legend. Never copy IDX's printed percentages: they have
been swapped before. Sectors (weekly page 7) and index contributions (page 5)
are also read from the rendered images, as in RPT-010 and RPT-018.
