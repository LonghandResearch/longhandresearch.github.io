## Notes for the reviewer

- **IDX's breadth labels.** On weekly p.4 the percentage labels under the
  first two segments of both breadth bars appear transposed (434 stocks is
  labelled 13% and 125 is labelled 45%; 434 of 963 is 45.1%). The page
  shows shares computed from the counts and says so in its method note.
- **Market cap total.** The breadth bar sums to Rp10,571tn, not the
  Rp10,627tn average market cap on p.1; the page calls 59.2% a share "of the
  total in IDX's breakdown".
- **The Rp50 cluster.** Eight of the ten weekly losers started the week at
  Rp50. Neither file says why, and the page says so instead of naming a
  cause. If an IDX announcement explains it (for example a change to the
  minimum price), it could be added with that announcement as its source.
- **Prior weeks.** The four-week total and last week's value per share rely
  on figures checked in `audits/QA-002.md`, not re-read this week.
