## 8. Method for the argument

The explanations table follows the analysis of competing hypotheses in Heuer, *Psychology of
Intelligence Analysis*, chapter 8. Six explanations are tested against the evidence that would rule each
out, and none survives alone. The paragraphs follow the claim, reasons, evidence and acknowledgement
pattern of Booth and colleagues, *The Craft of Research*. The check of last week's call follows Galef's
*Scout Mindset*: the planning case held, the relief case failed, and the claim that buybacks and state
buying might support prices is reported as not supported.
