## Forecast method and its limits

The planning window is four to twelve weeks from 19 October. It is an analyst
monitoring horizon, not a timing estimate generated by the formula.

Valuation sensitivity uses `I = 5185.105 * (1 + earningsPct/100) /
(1 + 11.03 * yieldBps/10000)`. IDX market P/E is a proxy and does not reproduce
IHSG's constituent earnings, payout or weights. Earnings-yield changes are
assumptions, not observed changes in the discount rate.

| Case | Earnings endpoints | Yield shifts, bp | Calculated endpoints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unresolved reform | -5%, 0% | +100, 0 | 4436.5034225, 5185.105 |
| Repair | 0%, +5% | -50, -100 | 5487.7546701, 6119.3214005 |
| Escalation | -15%, -10% | +250, +150 | 3454.7044876, 4004.1138616 |

No probabilities or weighted target. The interactive curves use earnings
0%, -10% and +5%, across -50 to +250 bp. Values and selected readouts derive from
the same pure arithmetic. The baseline guide marks the 16 October index anchor.

Capacity sensitivity uses hypothetical $1tn non-overlapping EM tracker assets,
September weight 0.43%, JISDOR 18,052. Sale $4.30bn = Rp77.6236tn. Divide by
Rp10.219402871525tn times assumed participation: 75.9571 sessions at 10%,
half that at 20%. These are market-wide turnover equivalents, not liquidation
dates. Current tracker assets, post-crash weights, overlapping mandates,
constituent liquidity, queue depth, competing sales and impact coefficients are
not established. Nothing converts the hypothetical sale into an IHSG price fall.
