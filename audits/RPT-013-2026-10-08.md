### Analytical frameworks (report pages 3 and 7-9)

The longer narrative distinguishes general mechanisms and conditional research
interpretations from measured outcomes in this period. Three additional
primary documents support that explanation without adding new numerical
observations:

- [Federal Reserve monetary-policy transmission primer](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/monetary-policy-what-are-its-goals-how-does-it-work.htm),
  "How changes in the federal funds rate affect the broader economy":
  expectations influence longer-term rates; financing terms influence housing
  and investment decisions; fixed and floating loan exposures differ.
- [Federal Reserve asset-valuation framework](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/may-2021-asset-valuations.htm),
  footnote 1 and valuation discussion: discounting future cash flows, safe
  rates and risk premiums. The report's stock-market interpretation is
  conditional on cash flows and risk premiums, not a prediction of prices.
- [The Fed Explained, monetary-policy chapter](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pf/pdf/pf_3.pdf),
  "Other Factors Affecting Monetary Policy", printed page 30: effects arrive
  with a lag; other influences on spending, output and inflation matter.

These mechanisms do not identify the current Treasury shock or quantify its
contribution to spending. The three paths in the assessment are conditions
to test, without assigned probabilities or forecasts. Country-specific
Indonesian effects remain unestablished.
