## Why SELL

The site's rating key (`about.html`) says SELL means downside of more than 10%.

| Measure | Value | Against the key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12-month target against price | 350.82 / 406.13 - 1 = -13.6% | More than 10% downside |
| Same, adding the assumed dividend of US$5.89 per ADS | -12.2% | Still more than 10% |
| Today's DCF value against price | 324.97 / 406.13 - 1 = -20.0% | Supports the call |
| Bear, base, bull | US$204, US$351, US$579 | Base is below the price, bull is above it |

The call is a valuation call, not a view that the business is weak. The report says so on its last page.
It also names what would change it: a lower price, or growth running about three points a year above the
base path. At a 9.5% WACC the target would be about US$403, so the result depends on the discount rate.
The 10.5% WACC is a stated judgment, not a regression.

3. The vendor series and TSMC's audited FY2025 figures differ. The valuation does not use the FY2025 cash figures.
4. `model_v2.py` reads the vendor workbook from the owner's computer and is kept here for the method, not as a runnable
   build. `dcf_replica.py` carries its inputs and runs on its own.
