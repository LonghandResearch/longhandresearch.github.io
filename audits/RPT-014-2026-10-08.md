## Scenario arithmetic

Every default is an illustrative analyst assumption: 100 Mt/year, USD60/t
realised selling price, USD35/t cash production cost, USD8/t inland/loading,
10% hypothetical revenue-linked charge. Not a company/sector average, current
royalty rate, tax determination or IEA forecast.

- Gross revenue, USD bn = volume Mt × selling price USD/t / 1,000.
- Revenue-linked charge = revenue × assumed percentage / 100.
- Production/inland cost, USD bn = volume × (production + inland) / 1,000.
- Cash contribution = revenue less both deductions.
- Break-even selling price = (production + inland) / (1 - charge / 100).

Defaults reconcile to USD6.00bn revenue, USD0.60bn charge, USD4.30bn variable
cost and USD1.10bn contribution. Margin 18.3333% and break-even USD47.7778/t
are rounded only for display. At USD30/t, contribution is negative USD1.60bn.
At 20 Mt with remaining defaults unchanged, contribution is USD0.22bn.
The sensitivity grid varies price and volume independently by -30%, -15%,
0%, +15%, +30% around the current inputs while holding unit costs and charge
constant. It assigns no probabilities and makes no EBITDA, free-cash-flow,
dividend, equity-value or stock-rating claim. Ocean freight, fixed overhead,
capex, income tax, working capital and financing are excluded explicitly.

Matched-period change checks: 495/517 - 1 = -4.255319%;
1,062/1,074 - 1 = -1.117318%; September price 147.1/135.2 - 1 = +8.801775%;
2025 annual price 108.4/136.1 - 1 = -20.352682%.
