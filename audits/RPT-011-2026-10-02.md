# RPT-011: large and actively traded stocks, 2 October 2026

This record covers the optional stock screen added to the 2 October IHSG Update. It is a dated description of size and trading depth, not an assessment that any share is safe or a recommendation to buy it. The report's official IHSG, market-wide foreign-flow and index-contribution figures continue to come from IDX Daily and Weekly Statistics.

## Sources and selection

- Flowless local terminal at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, `/api/health` latest trade date `2026-10-02`, runtime checkout `182fe27` (clean worktree when checked). The terminal's `/api/screener?sort_by=market_cap&sort_dir=desc&limit=200` supplied the frozen readings in `weekly/flowless-2026-10-02.json`. `/api/ticker/{ticker}` supplied daily series for independent arithmetic checks. The terminal uses IDX's Daily Trading Summary (`GetStockSummary`) feed. Its code defines market cap as shares outstanding times close, ADTV20 as the mean of daily `Value` over 20 sessions, and estimated foreign net as the sum of (foreign buy shares minus foreign sell shares) times each day's close over five sessions.
- Owner-supplied `ihsg 2.pdf`, IDX Daily Statistics for Friday 2 October 2026, page 4: top stocks by market capitalisation, rounded to whole Rp trillion. Page 3 lists daily top stocks by value; those figures are **not** used as the terminal's daily `Value` series because the figures differ and their venue scope has not been reconciled.
- Owner-supplied `ihsg week ly.pdf`, IDX Weekly Statistics for 28 September to 2 October 2026, page 5: IHSG index movers. The four names below identified as top-ten laggards are AMMN, TLKM, BBCA and BBRI.
- [IDX Statistics archive](https://www.idx.id/en/market-data/statistical-reports/statistics) is the public entry point for the two dated PDFs. The frozen Flowless readings are published with the report so readers can see the exact inputs; the terminal's code repository is private.

Selection at the 2 October close: market cap at least Rp10tn, ADTV20 at least Rp50bn, no zero-trade days in the latest 20 sessions, and `is_illiquid = false`. Sort by market cap and take five. The Rp50bn trading-depth line is Flowless's disclosed `LIQ_VACUUM_DEEP_ADTV_IDR`, while its own basic `is_illiquid` flag uses a much lower Rp1bn line or more than three no-trade sessions. Applying both is deliberate. Among the largest excluded names, DCII had Rp478.89tn cap but Rp0.11bn ADTV20, BYAN Rp391.67tn and Rp38.72bn, and BREN Rp378.62tn and Rp30.05bn. This shows why cap alone was not used.

## Interpretation limits

- The five-day foreign readings in Flowless are close-price-weighted estimates from foreign share volumes. They are not IDX's published foreign **value** and must not be added to or reconciled with the official weekly market-wide Rp5.04tn net-selling figure.
- The Flowless stock-summary daily `Value` series is not identical to the IDX Daily Statistics page 3 top-value table. For example, Flowless's 2 October BBCA value is Rp798.14bn, while the PDF prints Rp833bn. The venue/coverage difference is unresolved. The report therefore names ADTV20 as a **Flowless stock-summary measure** and does not present it as the official all-market IDX daily value.
- Size and trading depth say nothing by themselves about balance-sheet strength, valuation, governance, price risk or future returns. The report uses “large and actively traded,” never “safe.”
