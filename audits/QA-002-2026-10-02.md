The breadth graphic on weekly p.4 appears to transpose the labels for its
first two segments: 434/963 is 45.1%, not 13%, and 125/963 is 13.0%, not
45%. Its capitalisation labels appear similarly transposed. The report's
table uses the counts and recomputed shares and says so in the method note.
The breadth-bar capitalisation total (Rp10,571tn) also differs from the
p.1 market-cap figure (Rp10,627tn); the text explicitly calls it the
"total in IDX's breakdown".

## Corrections made in this review

1. Removed the four-week foreign-flow claim, prior-week value per share and
   prior-week negotiated value. They depend on 11, 18 and 25 September weekly
   files that were not among the owner's supplied IDX files. The previous
   week's net sale and activity comparisons retained here appear on the
   supplied 2 October weekly PDF itself.
2. Reworded claims that GOTO *explained* the week-on-week gap, regional
   losses *explained* the IHSG move, or Friday lacked broader buying. The
   cited tables establish observations and contributions, not those causes.
3. Added dated BI and Fed policy context from each institution's own press
   release and kept it separate from the market attribution.
4. Clarified the Rp0.01bn daily-versus-weekly flow discrepancy and used
   "stocks in IDX's breadth breakdown" rather than assuming the graphic
   includes every listed stock.
5. Aligned the catalogue summary with the corrected report. The catalogue
   blurb, title, date, category, null rating and sitemap URL already matched
   the primary data and the published page.
