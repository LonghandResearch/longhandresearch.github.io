04 / Investment interpretation

Watch the conversion from plans to cash.

For operators, the evidence that would strengthen the thesis is a dated service opening, disclosed delivered capacity, customer absorption and cash generation after expansion spending. A campus announcement by itself establishes none of those outcomes.

For industrial estates, the useful checkpoints are signed land sales, collections and remaining infrastructure commitments. For connectivity and power companies, look for contracts that identify the supplier, economics and delivery obligations. A nearby project is not enough to attribute future profit to a listed company.

Our interpretation remains conditional: more capacity under construction makes delivery evidence increasingly relevant. The price changes shown here do not establish better or worse project economics, and lower share prices alone do not establish cheap valuations.

The models retain their source dates. The September financial series, share counts, DCF and Monte Carlo assumptions remain in the original report. This supplement does not recalculate them using stale financial inputs.

The archived power calculator uses a Q3 electricity-tariff assumption. The PLN document list reviewed for this update did not establish a Q4 tariff. An October tariff should therefore not be inferred from that calculator.

For power, cooling and operating-cost scenarios, read AI Runs on Power and Water, which separates capacity, utilization, PUE and site-specific resource assumptions.
